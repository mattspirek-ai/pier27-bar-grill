About

Pier 27 Bar & Grill sits along Junction Highway in Kerrville — a neighborhood bar & grill with that Guadalupe riverside feel folks around here know. Deck vibes, good company, and a place to unwind after a day on (or near) the water.

Glad to be open again and glad to have you. Come as you are — locals, river rats, and first-timers welcome.
