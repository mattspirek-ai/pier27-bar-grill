About

Pier 27 Bar & Grill sits along Junction Highway in Kerrville — a neighborhood hangout with that Guadalupe riverside feel folks around here know. Deck vibes, cold drinks, good company, and a place to unwind after a day on (or near) the water.

Locals, river rats, and first-timers welcome. Come as you are.

Hours & specials

We’re often open around 11 AM – midnight, but hours and weekly specials can change. For today’s hours and what’s on, check Facebook — that’s where we post updates.
